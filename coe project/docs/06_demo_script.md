# 06. 3-Minute Demo Video Script & Storyboard

## 1. Video Overview & Production Details
- **Target Duration**: 3 minutes (180 seconds)
- **Target Audience**: Academic mentors, engineering leadership, and prospective learners
- **Core Message**: *Self-directed learners can navigate complex degree prerequisites and career trade-offs safely, transparently, and explainably with zero punitive surveillance.*

---
